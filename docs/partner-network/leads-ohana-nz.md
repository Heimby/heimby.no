### 1.7 Density read (32-unit operation)

1. **Trades abundant and well-vetted; cleaning is the bottleneck** — contract **two** cleaning
   firms + a 2–3-person Goodnest/Builderscrack individual bench, because a single-provider
   Saturday failure has no market-rate substitute.
2. **Handyman market is liquid but adversely selected at the top of Google** — anchor on
   platform-rated operators (Audesia; Handymen Services/Quickfix for assembly callouts), treat
   Builderscrack as the overflow queue, ignore SEO shells.
3. **Geography splits CBD vs Hutt (Petone holds the deep trade firms)** — one primary per
   category *per side* beats one region-wide vendor: sub-20-min CBD response, no cross-harbour
   travel charges. Locksmith choice should follow the key-control product (Beveridge), not
   lowest callout price.

### 3.7 Channels + the housing reality

- **Facebook:** Lake Tekapo Community (primary — **staff-accommodation threads already run
  there**), Lake Tekapo Trading, Twizel Community, Fairlie Community Noticeboard;
  laketekaponz.co.nz/services accepts business listings.
- **Seasonal labor:** **Backpacker Job Board's live Mackenzie page** (competitor Pristine Touch
  already advertises there), SEEK "accommodation-provided — Lake Tekapo" category, and the
  **counter-seasonal Roundhill/Ohau ski crews** (winter Jun–Oct staff available for summer
  turnover peak, and vice versa).
- **The binding constraint is beds, not vendors** (verified via RNZ/local-democracy reporting):
  workers sleep in cars/campervans and commute up to 2.5 h; STR economics (~$90k/yr per
  Airbnb) have consumed the long-term rental stock; Tekapo's wastewater plant runs at ~3.3×
  design capacity, throttling new consents; a worker-accommodation development is planned at
  **Burkes Pass (~15 min away — watch item / possible partner)**; and the mayor has warned
  accommodation providers that *"a firmer approach is coming."* **Arriving with 52 STR units
  and no worker-housing contribution is an operational, political and reputational risk —
  housing the crew is both the capacity fix and the licence-to-operate move.**

### 3.8 Density read (52-unit operation)

1. **Local cleaning + trades exist and are better than the population suggests** — but all are
   micro-operators: contract 2–3 cleaning firms in parallel (Mad Clean + Pristine Touch first),
   anchor maintenance on Binns, take Foleys/Mackelec for 24 h trades — and still plan the
   housed crew (doc 06 §5.3) for peak.
2. **Three categories have no local supply:** spa/sauna servicing (one Timaru firm — retained
   SLA + in-house water chemistry), locksmithing (smart-lock it away), laundry (negotiate the
